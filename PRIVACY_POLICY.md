# Privacy Policy for HabitWorks

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026

HabitWorks ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use HabitWorks on Android.

---

## 1. No Accounts & Device-Only Personal Data

HabitWorks works completely offline without accounts, sign-in, passwords, or our own servers.

All personal habit data you create remains **locally on your device** in the app's private storage (Android Room database and DataStore preferences):
- Optional first name you enter for your home greeting
- Habits, categories, icons, schedules, targets, reminder times, and completion logs
- Daily notes, vacation/skip days, streak freezes, and theme preferences

We do **not** upload, collect, read, or store your habits, logs, or notes on any developer-controlled cloud server.

### Backups & Exports
- **Android Auto Backup:** If enabled in your device's Android settings, Android's built-in backup system may back up app data to your personal Google Drive backup strictly under Android's platform privacy controls.
- **Manual JSON Backup & CSV Export:** When you export a JSON backup or CSV progress sheet, the file is saved directly to the location you choose via Android's Storage Access Framework or share sheet.

---

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob & UMP Consent)

HabitWorks is 100% free and supported by ads served by **Google AdMob**, using Google's **User Messaging Platform (UMP)** SDK to request consent where required:
- Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the **Android Advertising ID**), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data to serve, personalize (if consented), and measure banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads.
- You can manage ad personalization preferences through the consent prompt (where applicable) or your Android device settings (*Settings > Google > Ads*).

---

## 3. Notifications (Optional)

If you turn on daily reminders or per-habit reminders, HabitWorks requests the Android notification permission (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS` on Android 13+) solely to schedule and display local reminder notifications via Android WorkManager on your device. No notification content leaves your device.

---

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion

- You can delete all app data at any time from **Settings → Delete all my data**, by clearing app storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling HabitWorks.
- Because we do not maintain user accounts or developer servers, there is no remote account data to delete.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

HabitWorks is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
